## Call for applications to the Swedish National Graduate School in Medical Bioinformatics, 2027

To all current and prospective first-year PhD students studying Bioinformatics,

MedBioInfo, the Swedish National Graduate School in Medical Bioinformatics, will take applications for an incoming class in 2027, with application deadline of 28 February 2027.

MedBioInfo was established in 2017 to provide advanced training in bioinformatics to the next generation of life science researchers. Admitted students join a competitively selected national community. The affiliated faculty include many of the leading bioinformaticians in Sweden. Our host university is the  Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences. 

Most courses require a one-week at-home pre-assignment, one week in residence, and often a take-home post-assignment. Students typically take two courses per year for three years. The programme also includes an annual meeting with faculty talks, soft-skills workshops, student presentations and feedback.

### Eligibility

You are eligible to apply if you began or will begin your PhD between January 2025 and July 2026, with some flexibility. The target group is PhD students in life science doing primarily computational research. Selection is based on scientific and technical aptitude. Your PI is required to pay tuition for three years, tuition was 20000kr/year from 2024 to 2026, the 2027 tuition level has not been set.

### Why MedBioInfo remains attractive for PIs and students

1. Admitted students get a full menu of bioinformatics courses, delivered on a schedule made with the student in mind.
2. Courses have been refined over multiple years through evaluation and follow-up.
3. Admission signals strong suitability for computational coursework and gives a distinction recognised by employers.
4. Networking and mentorship e.g. through the annual meeting is as important as the courses themselves.

The application system is not yet open.
