Courses and Events

All courses funded by this research school make their material freely available online using a Creative Commons licence. Lectures may be recorded and shared online.

Compulsory courses (3–5 HP)

The basis of the research school is two courses per year. Later courses may assume mastery of concepts taught earlier, so students are encouraged to take them in sequence.

First-year courses

Reproducible Research 3 HP

Samuel Coulbourn Flores, Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences

Introduces reproducible bioinformatics practice, including containers, version management, pipelining, Unix, and good scientific computing habits.

In-person 28 May – 1 June 2018 at SciLifeLab, Solna
Online 11–12 and 15–17 June 2020
Online 14–18 June 2021
In-person 13–17 June 2022 at SLU Ultuna, Uppsala
In-person 29 May – 2 June 2023 at SLU Ultuna, Uppsala
In-person 27–31 May 2024 at SLU Ultuna, Uppsala
In-person 6–10 October 2025 at SLU Ultuna, Uppsala
In-person 14–18 September 2026 at SLU Ultuna, plus pre-assignments. There are hybrid help sessions 8–12 September which are optional for MedBioInfo students, and expected for GS-VMAS students

Statistics in Bioinformatics 3 HP

Dirk Repsilber, Örebro University and Zelmina Lubovac, University of Skövde

Covers univariate and multivariate statistics, omics data analysis, and predictive biosignatures for precision medicine.

Online 19–23 October 2020
In-person 22–26 November 2021 at University of Skövde
In-person 21–25 November 2022 at Örebro University
In-person 20–24 November 2023 at Örebro University
In-person 24–28 November 2025 at Örebro University
In-person 23–27 November 2026 at Örebro University

Second- or third-year courses

Experimental Design for High-throughput Technologies 3 HP

Carsten Daub, Karolinska Institutet

Focuses on experimental design for high-throughput studies and critical evaluation of design choices in bioinformatics workflows. Now a DDLS course, funded partly by MedBioInfo. Planned to run yearly.

In-person 3–7 September 2018 at SciLifeLab, Solna
In-person 2–6 September 2019 at SciLifeLab, Solna
In-person 25–29 October 2021 at SciLifeLab, Solna
Hybrid 19–23 and 26–30 September 2022
In-person 13–17 November 2023 at SciLifeLab, Solna
In-person: 3–7 November 2025 at SciLifeLab, Solna
Planned, in-person: 23–27 November 2026 at SciLifeLab, Solna

Deep Learning 5 HP

Chun-Biu Li, Stockholm University

An advanced course in deep learning, funded by DDLS, with seats reserved for MedBioInfo students. We are planning to run this course yearly.

Ran: onsite 8–12 June 2026

Computational Systems Biology 3–5 HP

Mika Gustafsson, Linköping University

Introduces systems biology concepts and mathematical models for biomedical research, from small-scale to large-scale modelling. This course runs every other year from 2026.

Ran 5–8 May 2025 in Linköping
Ran: 4–8 May 2026 in Linköping
Planned: sometime in 2028 in Linköping

Career Alternatives and Your Scientific CV 1 HP

Carsten Daub, Karolinska Institutet

Explores academic and especially industry careers, with exercises on CV writing and interviews.

First ran: 26-27 May 2026

Annual meetings

Another major part of the research school is the annual meeting. These meetings combine student presentations, faculty talks, mentoring, and soft-skills sessions, while also helping students build a long-term national scientific network. Students in years 1-3 are automatically invited, 4th year students please contact the Dean of Students to join.

Recommended course order

Students are recommended to take courses in the following order.

Admitted in 2026: Year 1: Reproducible Research and Statistics. Year 2: Experimental Design for High-throughput Technologies, and Deep Learning. Year 3: Computational Systems Biology and Career Alternatives and Your Scientific CV.
Admitted in 2027: Year 1: Reproducible Research, and Statistics. Year 2: Computational Systems Biology, and Experimental Design for High-throughput Technologies. Year 3: Deep Learning, and Career Alternatives and Your Scientific CV.

Completion certificate

We issue the MedBioInfo completion certificate upon request to students who complete the program. In some cases we may ask you to send in your course and annual meeting certificates. The certificate is not a formal degree, and does not confer HP.

You can earn your certificate by completing either of the following:

Five courses and one annual meeting.
Four courses and two annual meetings.
